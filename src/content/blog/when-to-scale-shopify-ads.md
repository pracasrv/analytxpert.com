# When to Scale Shopify Ads (And When to Kill Them)

Most Shopify brands scale ads based on ROAS. That's a mistake.

Here's the framework I use with my clients to decide what to scale and what to kill.

## The Decision Framework

### ✅ Scale if:
1. **Positive contribution margin** (not just positive ROAS)
2. **Repeat purchase rate ≥ 20%** (you're buying customers, not one-time shoppers)
3. **Return rate < 15%** (or your category average)
4. **Stable over 30+ days** (not a temporary spike)

### ❌ Kill if:
1. **Negative contribution margin** after 7 days
2. **Return rate > 30%**
3. **Repeat purchase rate < 10%** (you're buying bad customers)
4. **High AOV driven entirely by upsells** (core offer isn't profitable)

### ⏸ Pause & investigate if:
1. Contribution margin is break-even or slightly positive
2. ROAS looks great but something feels off
3. Volume is high but cash flow is tight

## Real Example: "Scale" Campaign

**Campaign metrics:**
- ROAS: 4.2x
- Spend: $8k/day
- Revenue: $33.6k/day

But after calculating contribution margin:
- COGS: 35% ($11.7k)
- Shipping: $2.8k
- Fees: $1k
- Returns (tracked with 2-week lag): 22% = $7.4k

**Net contribution: $2.7k/day** — contribution margin: 8%

### Decision: Pause & Fix

What I did:
1. Analyzed which SKUs in the campaign had high return rates
2. Paused ad sets promoting those SKUs
3. Adjusted creative to set better expectations (reducing returns)
4. Relaunched with tighter targeting

**Result:** Contribution margin went from 8% to 18% at the same spend level.

## The Bottom Line

Don't scale ROAS. Scale contribution margin.
