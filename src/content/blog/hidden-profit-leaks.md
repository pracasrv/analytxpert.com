# 7 Hidden Profit Leaks Killing Your Shopify Store

Running a profitable Shopify store isn't just about driving more traffic. Sometimes the biggest gains come from plugging the leaks you didn't know existed.

## 1. Returns Lag

**The Problem:** Returns can take 2-4 weeks to show up in your data. By the time you realize a product or campaign has a 25% return rate, you've already spent thousands scaling it.

**The Fix:** Track return rates by SKU and acquisition source in real-time, not retroactively.

## 2. Attribution Errors

**The Problem:** Meta and Google both want to take credit for the same sale. You think you're profitable on both channels, but you're double-counting revenue.

**The Fix:** Use platform-agnostic attribution. Track conversions at the order level, not the platform level.

## 3. Unprofitable "Winners"

**The Problem:** Your best-selling SKU might be your biggest profit drain if it has high COGS, frequent returns, or attracts discount-hunters.

**The Fix:** Calculate contribution margin by SKU. Kill or restructure offers around unprofitable products.

## 4. Post-Purchase Upsells Masking Problems

**The Problem:** Your AOV looks great because of checkout upsells, but the core offer is unprofitable. If the upsell acceptance rate drops, your margins collapse.

**The Fix:** Measure profitability of the core offer separately from upsells.

## 5. Shipping Cost Creep

**The Problem:** Your "free shipping" threshold was set a year ago. Since then, shipping costs went up 15%, but your AOV didn't.

**The Fix:** Audit your actual shipping costs monthly and adjust thresholds or pricing accordingly.

## 6. Slow-Moving Inventory

**The Problem:** You're holding $50k in inventory that hasn't moved in 90 days. That's cash sitting idle—and often becoming obsolete.

**The Fix:** Track inventory turn rate by SKU. Liquidate or bundle slow movers aggressively.

## 7. Low Repeat Purchase Rate on New Customers

**The Problem:** You're acquiring customers at $60 CAC assuming 2x LTV. But if those customers don't return, you're underwater.

**The Fix:** Track repeat purchase rate by cohort and acquisition source. Focus on channels that bring repeat buyers, not just one-time shoppers.
